# Sheru Bhai Animal-Man Stories

## Purpose
Generate original Hindi short-form stories with cinematic animal-headed humanoid characters, suspense, emotional family/business conflict, and a strong reveal or cliffhanger.

## Audio rule
- Do not call any voice, narration, speech synthesis, or voice-typing provider for this category.
- Select only a playable Music Library track whose status is Active and rights status is Owned or Authorized.
- Use that uploaded master track as the soundtrack. Do not substitute YouTube/Spotify URLs for the master audio file.
- If no playable eligible master track exists, fail clearly and ask for the original audio to be added to Music Library.

## Story rules
- Characters have humanoid bodies and naturally integrated animal heads (Lion Man, Tiger Man, Lion Cub Man, Tiger Cub Man, etc.).
- Keep species, fur/markings, face, outfit, accessories, and proportions consistent between scenes.
- Use a strong first-scene hook, escalating conflict, expressive reactions, suspense, and a satisfying twist or cliffhanger.
- Stories and story summaries are in Hindi. Scene visual directions can be structured clearly for the renderer.
- Do not copy existing franchise characters or stories.
- No narration or generated speech audio. Music is the only audio track.

## Automation
- Category name: Sheru Bhai Animal-Man Stories
- Category ID: animal_stories
- Default daily slot: 23:00 Asia/Kolkata
- Format: YouTube Short, vertical 9:16
- Publishing: send the rendered result through the existing Buffer publishing route and preserve the normal connected-channel configuration.
