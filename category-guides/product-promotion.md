# Product Promotion — AI Production Playbook

## Mission
Explain a real product/service clearly and persuasively without inventing features or promising impossible outcomes.

## Before writing
Identify exact product, target audience, audience problem, verified features, price/date/region if supplied, limitations, CTA and source of each factual claim. Separate verified facts from opinions. If product details are missing, ask for them or create a transparent general concept without made-up specifications.

## Video structure (20–45 seconds for short form)
1. Hook: audience problem or relevant use case.
2. Product introduction: what it is and who it may help.
3. 2–3 verified benefits with practical examples.
4. Important limitation/eligibility if material.
5. Clear, honest CTA.

## Visuals
Show the real product/UI where available; do not fake dashboards, reviews, sales figures, certifications or testimonials. Use readable labels, clear demonstrations and accessible captions. Disclose sponsorship/affiliate relationships when applicable.

## Metadata and QA
Title should mention product and real benefit; description includes key details and CTA. Use 3–5 relevant hashtags. Check claims, spelling, prices, link, platform crop and rights. No guaranteed income, health, performance or popularity claims unless substantiated and appropriately qualified.
