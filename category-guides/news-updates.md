# News & Updates — AI Production Playbook

## Mission
Make a timely, accurate, neutral Hindi-first explainer that helps viewers understand what happened and why it matters.

## Research rules
- Research before scripting. Prefer primary/official sources and reputable independent reporting.
- Confirm publication date and event date separately. Seek a second reliable source for important claims.
- If facts conflict, say so and do not present uncertain claims as fact. Never invent current events, names, quotes, numbers or causes.
- Keep a source ledger: claim, source title, URL, publication date, what it supports. If live research is unavailable, do not label the story “latest/today”; choose evergreen context or stop and report the limitation.

## Short-video structure (about 30–60 seconds unless requested otherwise)
1. 0–3s: specific factual hook, no misleading sensationalism.
2. 3–10s: what happened, when and where.
3. 10–35s: verified context and why it matters.
4. Final seconds: what is confirmed, what remains unknown, source attribution / neutral question.

## Visuals and editing
Use maps, timelines, simple labels, licensed/authorized photos or original graphics. Do not fabricate documentary footage, falsely depict a real person/event, or use unrelated stock visuals as proof. Add readable Hindi captions and source/date on screen where useful.

## Metadata and QA
Title states the actual event and key subject; avoid clickbait, fear-bait and unsupported claims. Description summarizes facts and lists sources. Use 3–5 topic-specific hashtags only. Verify names, dates, numbers, source links, spelling, captions and rights before scheduling.
