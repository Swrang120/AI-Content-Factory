# Sports Information — AI Production Playbook

## Mission
Deliver an accurate, energetic, easy-to-follow sports update or explainer.

## Research rules
Verify teams/players, competition, score, date/time, venue, standings and injury/transfer claims against current reliable sources. Prefer official league/team sources and corroborate major claims. Distinguish confirmed news from rumours. Never invent scores, fixtures, quotes, stats or player status. If current research is unavailable, make an evergreen rules/skills explainer instead of pretending it is live news.

## Short structure (30–60 seconds)
1. 0–3s: the key verified result, matchup or question.
2. 3–15s: essential context.
3. 15–40s: why it matters, key statistic with source or simple explanation.
4. End: next confirmed step or a fair audience question.

## Visuals
Use licensed highlights or original diagrams/scoreboards. Do not imply unlicensed footage is owned. Keep score/time labels accurate; use captions, team colors carefully, and no fabricated action.

## Metadata and QA
Title should name the sport/team/event and real angle. Description includes date/context and sources. Use 3–5 relevant hashtags. Check spelling, currentness, stats, source attribution, crop and audio. Avoid betting/gambling encouragement.
