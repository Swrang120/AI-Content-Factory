# Music Promotion — AI Production Playbook

## Mission
Promote the user's authorized original song by finding the strongest short-form moment, not merely placing the full song over a generic template.

## Required analysis before editing
1. Confirm the selected audio is the original authorized master. Never treat Spotify/YouTube watch URLs as playable master audio.
2. Inspect audio duration and, where tools support it, waveform/energy changes, vocal entry, beat drops, chorus/refrain, instrumental breaks and phrase boundaries.
3. Transcribe with timestamps if possible. Do not invent lyrics. If transcription/audio analysis fails, mark the analysis as limited and do not claim the chosen line is proven best.
4. Compare candidate sections across the song. Score emotional impact, instant comprehension, memorability, melody/vocal payoff (only if actually analyzed), sing-along potential, relatable feeling, early hook, clean start/end and suitability to 9:16.
5. Select one strongest 12–30 second cut and keep at least one alternate candidate for testing. Avoid cutting words, breaths unnaturally or musical phrases mid-flow. Respect any rights/permissions.
6. Build the visual style to match the real mood: romantic, sad, folk, energetic, devotional etc. Do not assume every song is sad/romantic. Use readable on-screen text, restrained motion, high contrast, and artist/song identity.
7. Use only a short lyric excerpt actually present in the supplied transcript and allowed for the user's own song. Never fabricate lyrics.
8. Create a truthful, distinct title, caption/description and 3–5 relevant hashtags in the selected language. Avoid fake “viral/trending” claims. Example hashtags must fit the song's actual language/genre.
9. Before publish, QA audio sync, clip start/end, crop, title spelling, artist credit, loudness/clipping, text safe margins and destination format.

## Output
Return: track facts; analysis coverage/limitations; candidate cuts with timestamps and reason; chosen cut + reason; alternate cut; visual concept; title; description/caption; 3–5 hashtags; QA status. Never imply audience trend prediction without actual platform analytics.

## Learning loop
When real post metrics exist, compare retention/watch time/completion/shares/saves/comments by hook, clip duration, mood, title and format. Use evidence to improve future choices; views alone are not enough.
