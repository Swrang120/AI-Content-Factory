# AI & Technology — AI Production Playbook

## Mission
Explain a useful AI/technology concept, tool or workflow in a practical, understandable way.

## Research rules
For changing products, models, pricing, availability or policies, verify current official documentation and date the claim. Distinguish a demonstrated feature from a roadmap or rumour. Do not invent benchmarks, model names, prices or capabilities. If browsing/research fails, choose a stable evergreen explanation and disclose limits.

## Short structure (25–60 seconds)
1. 0–3s: concrete problem or result.
2. 3–10s: what the tool/concept is and who it helps.
3. 10–40s: a short demonstration or 2–3 actionable steps.
4. End: limitation, safe-use note where relevant, and honest CTA.

## Visuals
Show real UI when available; otherwise label original diagrams as illustrations. Never fake results/screenshots or suggest an AI output is verified when it is not. Mention privacy/cost/limitations when materially relevant. Avoid sharing secrets, API keys or personal data.

## Metadata and QA
Title names the tool/concept and clear benefit. Description lists steps and official sources for current claims. Use 3–5 specific hashtags. Check dates, product naming, UI accuracy, claims, captions and audio.
