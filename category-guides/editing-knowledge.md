# Editing Knowledge — AI Production Playbook

## Mission
Teach one useful editing skill viewers can understand and try safely on their own project.

## Before scripting
Pick one specific outcome, such as smooth transitions, readable captions, color correction, audio cleanup or a workflow in CapCut/VN/Alight Motion/Premiere Pro. Confirm app/version details when relevant. Do not invent buttons or claim paid features are free.

## Short structure (20–45 seconds)
1. 0–3s: show the final result or state the editing problem.
2. 3–8s: list the tool/material needed.
3. 8–30s: demonstrate 2–4 clear steps with screen labels.
4. Final seconds: show before/after and a concise CTA.

## Visuals and audio
Prefer real screen recording or accurate original mock-ups. Zoom/crop to the control being discussed. Use large, readable Hindi captions, numbered steps and clear cursor/tap indication. Avoid clutter, excessive effects and background music that masks narration.

## Metadata and QA
Title includes the exact skill/tool and outcome; description lists steps/tools and any version caveat. Use 3–5 relevant hashtags. Test every instruction, verify UI labels, captions, audio levels, aspect ratio and final export before publishing.
